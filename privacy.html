Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

StarrySound (“the app”) is an Android music player. This policy explains what data the app handles. The app has no backend server operated by us: we do not receive, store or have access to your data.

Data stored on your device

Playlists, liked songs, play history, listening statistics, settings and cached files are stored locally on your phone. You can remove them by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it.

Google sign-in (optional)

If you sign in with Google, the app receives your email address and display name to show which account is connected. Access tokens are managed by Google Play services and are not sent anywhere except to Google’s own APIs. You can use the app without signing in. Each permission is requested only when the related feature is used:

  • Google Drive – app data folder (drive.appdata): the app uploads and downloads one backup file (playlists, liked songs, YouTube play history, listening statistics) in a hidden folder that only this app can access. API keys you enter are never uploaded. The app cannot see your other Drive files with this permission.
  • Google Drive – read only (drive.readonly): used only by the “Drive” music tab to list and play audio files from your Drive. Files are downloaded to the app’s cache on your device. Nothing in Drive is changed.
  • YouTube – read only (youtube.readonly): used to show your YouTube playlists and, if you turn it on, to read your liked music videos and subscriptions so the app can improve recommendations on your device. Nothing in your account is changed.
  • YouTube – manage (youtube): requested only if you use “Convert” to copy a local playlist into a new private playlist in your YouTube account.

Data obtained from Google APIs is used only to provide these user-facing features. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with third parties, and not used to train AI models. Humans do not read it. StarrySound’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can sign out in the app at any time, and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The backup file can be deleted from your Google account’s Drive app-data settings.

Optional third-party services

  • AI recommendations: only if you enter your own API key (Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic). Artist and song names from your likes and history, and any text you type for an AI playlist, are sent to the service you chose, under that service’s own terms.
  • Last.fm: only if you connect it. Listening history is recorded to your Last.fm account.
  • YouTube and other online sources: searching and playing songs contacts YouTube and similar public services from your device.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
