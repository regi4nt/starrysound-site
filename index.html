StarrySound

StarrySound is a free Android music player with an equalizer, a YouTube-based “Quick” feed, playlists, an alarm and a screensaver.

Features

  • Play songs from your device and from YouTube, with a built-in equalizer and audio effects.
  • Playlists, liked songs, listening statistics and song recommendations that stay on your phone.
  • Optional Google sign-in to back up and sync your playlists, likes, history and statistics to the hidden app-data folder of your own Google Drive, to browse the audio files in your Drive, and to read your YouTube playlists.

Your data

StarrySound has no server. Your data stays on your device and in your own Google account. See the Privacy Policy for details.
